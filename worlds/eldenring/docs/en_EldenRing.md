## What does randomization do in this game?

Items you can obtain across the Lands Between are shuffled into the Archipelago multiworld:
weapons, armor, talismans, sorceries and incantations, upgrade materials (smithing and somber
stones, Scadutree Fragments), crafting materials, consumables, and key items. The locations that
held those items instead send checks out to the rest of the multiworld, and the items you need to
progress arrive from other players' worlds.

Optionally, enemy and boss placements can be randomized as well.

## What is the goal of Elden Ring when randomized?

The goal is set by the **Ending Condition** option:

- **Final Boss** - defeat the final boss (Elden Beast, or the DLC's Promised Consort Radahn when
  the DLC is enabled).
- **Elden Beast** - defeat the Elden Beast; a shorter run with the DLC on.
- **All Remembrances** - defeat every (non-missable) remembrance boss.
- **All Bosses** - defeat every (non-missable) boss.
- **Capital** - reach Leyndell and defeat Morgott, the Omen King; a short run that pairs with a
  region count to seal off everything past the capital.
- **Messmer** - a DLC mini-campaign ending at Messmer the Impaler in Shadow Keep, keeping only the
  front half of the Realm of Shadow.
- **Godrick** - the shortest base-game run: keep Limgrave, the Weeping Peninsula and Stormveil
  Castle, then defeat Godrick the Grafted.

## How is the world gated?

The **World Logic** option controls how regions open up:

- **Region Lock** - each region requires a received key item to enter.
- **Region Bosses** - each region opens once a share of its bosses are defeated.
- **Region Lock Bosses** - both a key item and the boss requirement.
- **Open World** - no region gating; the map is open from the start.

With a lock-based logic you can also randomize the number of active regions (for shorter, more
focused seeds) and reorganize Sites of Grace so received region keys light a warp into the area
they unlock.

## What other changes can be made?

- **DLC (Shadow of the Erdtree)** support, including DLC-only modes.
- **Enemy and boss randomization**, with optional difficulty scaling.
- Quality-of-life adjustments to leveling, warps, and grace placement, depending on your options.
