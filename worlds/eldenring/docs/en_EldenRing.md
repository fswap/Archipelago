## What does randomization do in this game?

Items that the player can pick up across the Lands Between -- weapons, armor, talismans,
spells, upgrade materials, and key items -- are shuffled into the Archipelago multiworld.
Progress is gated by the items you receive from other players, and your own checks feed
items back out to them.

## What is the goal of Elden Ring when randomized?

Reach and defeat the configured ending boss. The required objective depends on your chosen
options (for example defeating Godrick, the Elden Beast, or completing a DLC campaign).

## What other changes are made to the game?

Depending on your options, regions may be locked behind received key items, graces and
warps may be reorganized, and enemy and boss placements may be randomized.
